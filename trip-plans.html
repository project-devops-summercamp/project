Trip Plan Guide

Curated, realistic itineraries with seasons, altitude, and travel timings in mind.

7-Day Northern Highlights (Hunza + Naran)

  • Day 1: Islamabad → Naran (7–9 hrs via Hazara). River Kunhar.
  • Day 2: Naran → Babusar Top (seasonal) → Hunza (6–8 hrs). Eagle’s Nest.
  • Day 3: Attabad Lake boats + Hussaini Bridge + Passu Cones.
  • Day 4: Baltit & Altit Forts; apricot dishes in Karimabad.
  • Day 5: Khunjerab Pass (if open) or Hopper Glacier.
  • Day 6: Return to Naran. Lake Saif‑ul‑Mulook jeep trip.
  • Day 7: Naran → Islamabad (8–10 hrs). Buffer day.

Season: Jun–Sep • Notes: Layers; fast‑changing conditions.

10-Day Heritage & Coast (Lahore → Karachi → Makran)

  • Days 1‑2: Lahore: Badshahi Mosque, Fort, Food Street.
  • Day 3: Fly/train to Karachi. Clifton sunset + seafood.
  • Day 4: Quaid’s Mausoleum, Mohatta, Empress Market.
  • Day 5: Churna Island snorkel or Manora beach.
  • Day 6: Karachi → Kund Malir (Hingol NP). Princess of Hope.
  • Day 7: Kund Malir → Gwadar. Hammerhead, Koh‑e‑Battil.
  • Day 8: Optional Astola Island (weather permitting).
  • Day 9: Return to Karachi with photo stops.
  • Day 10: Buffer/shopping.

Season: Nov–Mar • Notes: Fuel stops & advisories for MCH.

5-Day Swat Sampler (Malam Jabba + Kalam)

  • Day 1: Islamabad → Mingora (5–6 hrs). Fizagat Park.
  • Day 2: Malam Jabba chairlifts; view points & zip line.
  • Day 3: Mingora → Kalam (5–6 hrs). Ushu Forest.
  • Day 4: Mahodand Lake jeep trip (seasonal).
  • Day 5: Return to Islamabad.

Season: Apr–Oct • Notes: Mountain roads; prefer daylight driving.

3-Day Lahore Heritage & Food

  • Day 1: Badshahi Mosque, Fort, Walled City walking tour.
  • Day 2: Museum, Shalimar Gardens; night food tour (Gawalmandi/Anarkali).
  • Day 3: Wagah ceremony (PM). Shopping on MM Alam.

Season: Oct–Mar • Notes: Fridays crowded at Badshahi; dress modestly.
