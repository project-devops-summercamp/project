About SafarNama

SafarNama curates practical, up-to-date travel guidance for Pakistan—from Karakoram giants to the Makran coast. Our team blends local knowledge, verified timings, and responsible tourism tips so your journey is safe, authentic, and unforgettable.

  • Respect local customs; dress modestly at religious/heritage sites.
  • Leave no trace—pack out your trash and use refillable bottles.
  • Hire licensed guides and jeeps for high-altitude tracks.
